## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Disciplined system, product-unspecific screens. Zero off-palette classes, a worded level ramp, reduced motion, one focus ring. But Log is a flat list of identical cards, Insights is three stacked sections with "Show 48 more", and the food-property idea surfaces as 11px amber pills that make every meal look flagged.

**Deterministic scan:** Static scan 0 findings (verified not a broken scanner). Public pages: only cream-palette (the surface token, false positive) and an advisory em-dash count on privacy.
