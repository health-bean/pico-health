## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Copy is authored, layouts are generic. Backfill cues, honest counts, and the one-hue property scale are specific to Pico. The differentiator, food-property correlation, has no visual form: Insights is a long list of identical rows.

**Deterministic scan:** Static scan 0 findings (sanity-checked). Public pages: cream-palette on the surface token (false positive) and an advisory em-dash count on privacy. No overflow, exactly one h1, and no console errors on all 24 captures. Settings shows the stored protocol with Save disabled. Quick Add sheet on top of the tab bar with focus on its heading. No Insights row shows a percentage in its count. In-page: a thin-border-wide-shadow flag on the capture bar, width transitions on slider fills, 0px-padding false positive on a 44px min-height button, ~85-character protocol description on Settings at 1280. A11y: Chat and Reflect notes textareas have no accessible name; 11 of 12 controls inside Quick Add are under 44px (tabs 40px, chips and inputs 36px, Snack chip clipped); Settings About links 20px tall.
