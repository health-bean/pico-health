## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Authored for this product in exactly one place: the Log capture loop. CaptureBar, PendingCaptureCard, ShortcutRow, and ClarifierRow are unmistakably built for a foggy patient logging a home-cooked plate: a labelled "+ Add", rotating placeholders in the patient's own words, streaming "Reading…" state copy, editable chips, and a post-save clarifier that states its reason. The Log empty state, the Chat starters, and the plain-language legal pages are the other pockets of real authorship. Everywhere else is a competent generic health-SaaS shell that mySymptoms or Bearable could ship unchanged. "Botanical Clinical" amounts to Fraunces on a few headings, warm-tinted shadows, and teal on cream. There is no botanical motif, no illustration language, no signature component shape; EmptyState is the standard icon-tile-plus-two-lines default. The component layer contradicts the brand commitment: the Badge primitive is built on emerald/red/amber/purple, Button danger and Input errors on red, Reflect uses five hues, and Insights uses emoji as icons. Roughly 316 to 348 off-system Tailwind palette classes across 47 files and 14 inline style attributes, against a stated commitment of zero.

## Overall Impression

The capture loop is genuinely good and worth protecting as the reference for everything else. The rest of the app is assembled rather than designed, and the payoff screen actively harms the audience it serves: an anxious, foggy patient opening Insights meets hundreds of oldest-first "New pattern" cards, some of which pair symptoms with "good mood". The single biggest opportunity is to make Insights as authored and as quiet as Log.

## What's Working

- **The capture loop embodies the product principles in one component tree.** Labelled Add, teaching placeholders, streaming state copy, editable chips, undo-first, deterministic clarifiers with a stated why. Principles 1, 3, and 6 live here.
- **Reversibility is real.** No confirm dialogs on the main path; delete is a toast with Undo and a real restore; the Quick Add sheet refuses backdrop dismiss when items are selected; Reflect autosaves with a whispered "Saved".
- **Copy in the patient's register where it exists.** The Log empty state, Chat starters, progress-strip lines, and the legal pages' "Your data is yours" are paragraphs no other tracker could reuse.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P0] Insights alert stack is unbounded and oldest-first.** The alerts route orders by createdAt ascending with no limit, and AlertStack renders all of them above the curated Triggers/Helpers sections. 379 cards, 34,728px at 375. Some alerts contradict their own numbers ("Headache on 16 of 68 days with bell peppers (24%), vs 31% of days without" is a protective signal labelled "New pattern"), and some pair symptoms with "good mood". Why it matters: this is the payoff screen for an anxious-about-food patient, and it currently reads as noise that violates "observe, don't prescribe". Fix: newest-first with a server cap, show 3 with "N more" and a "Clear all", auto-expire alerts older than 30 days or absent from the current window, suppress alerts whose conditional rate is below the base rate, and move alerts below or into the curated sections as a "new this week" marker. Suggested command: /impeccable distill (Insights), then /impeccable shape if the alert concept is reconsidered.

2. **[P0] Zoom is disabled and motion is unconditional.** `maximumScale: 1` in app/layout.tsx (known), and zero `prefers-reduced-motion` handling while stagger animations run on every timeline card and every capture chip. Contrast also fails: the muted text token measures 3.06:1 on the surface and warm-400 measures 2.5:1, and no stylesheet contains a `:focus-visible` rule. Why it matters: the audience includes low vision, tremor, and vestibular sensitivity. Fix: remove maximumScale, add a reduced-motion block in globals.css for the five keyframes, darken `--color-text-muted` to at least 4.5:1 and stop using warm-400 for text, add focus-visible rings to nav links and the tab bar. Suggested command: /impeccable audit, then /impeccable harden.

3. **[P1] Off-protocol logging lectures the user.** ProtocolComplianceWarning shows "Protocol Compliance Warning" with a red "Proceed Anyway"; FoodTimelineCard shows an amber AlertTriangle "Protocol Warning / Nightshade not allowed" box that stays on the card forever. The capture path already does it right ("Outside your protocol · nightshade" in quiet warning text). Why it matters: PRODUCT.md says never lecture about off-protocol eating, and the daily loop currently ends on a warning instead of on "logged". Fix: replace both with the PendingCaptureCard treatment: a neutral inline tag, primary "Log it anyway", secondary "Pick another", no symptom threat. Suggested command: /impeccable clarify.

4. **[P1] Backfilling on Log is unsafe.** The day fetch has no abort or sequence guard (a stale response rendered "Nothing logged this day" for Apr 24, which has 13 entries); Quick Add receives no date, so picking from a list while viewing a past day silently logs to today; jumping back N days is N 40px chevron taps at the top of the screen. Why it matters: backfilling at 11pm is the stated usage scene, and a foggy user who sees a logged day appear empty concludes the app lost their data. Fix: AbortController in fetchEntries, pass the selected date into QuickAddSheet and default "When" to it, and make the date heading open a week strip (Reflect already has one). Suggested command: /impeccable harden.

5. **[P1] Reintroductions header overflows the phone and has two primaries.** Two side-by-side buttons in a justify-between header push scroll width to 478px at 375, and the fixed bottom nav stretches with it. Fix: stack on mobile, make "Start New" the sole primary, demote "View Recommendations" to a text link. Suggested command: /impeccable adapt.

6. **[P1] Log Out is the loudest element on Settings.** Full-width danger-red button with no confirm, plus a second 36px logout in the header on every page. A mis-tap by a foggy user means re-authenticating. Fix: outline variant inside the Account card, drop the header logout. Suggested command: /impeccable distill.

7. **[P2] The color system leaks at the primitive layer.** Badge variants on emerald/red/amber/purple, Button danger and form errors on red, a five-hue score slider, red/amber/emerald section chrome and left-border rails in Insights, emoji icons in Insights and PropertyPatternCard. The semantic tokens `--color-success/warning/danger/info` exist and are used in about three places. Fix: rebuild Badge and the error/danger states on the semantic tokens, sweep the 47 files, make property levels a single-hue ramp, replace emoji with Lucide. Suggested command: /impeccable colorize, then /impeccable polish.

8. **[P2] Typography and card fragmentation.** Fraunces display on Log/Insights/Settings cards/login; bold sans h1 on Reflect, Reintroductions, signup, forgot, reset, and Settings. Four tab-control styles. Two timeline card chromes (bordered food card with emerald icon vs shadowed generic card with teal icon), each carrying a type badge that duplicates the type icon. 10px labels in the tab bar; 9 to 11px micro-type in InsightRow, HelperRow, PatternCard; 94-character measure on Insights body text. Fix: one PageTitle primitive, one Tabs primitive, one timeline card chrome without the badge, 11px floor, 65 to 75 character measure. Suggested command: /impeccable typeset, then /impeccable layout.

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (one-handed, distracted mobile user):** Header logout at 36px and the day chevrons at 40px sit top-right, so every backfill step is a thumb stretch. Quick Add close is 32px at the top of the sheet. Alert dismiss is a 22px target, 379 times. Insights range toggles are about 24px tall. Admins get six 10px-label tabs in the bottom bar.

**Jordan (first-timer):** Onboarding sends them to Chat to log: WelcomeStep says "through simple conversation" and ReadyStep pairs "chat with your AI coach" with logging examples, contradicting the 2026-09-02 decision that capture lives on Log. Skipping the protocol step leaves Settings reading "No protocol selected" and Reintroductions blocking with "Please select a protocol first". The Settings "Free Plan / Upgrade for AI insights" card implies Insights is paywalled when it is not. "Early signal" and "COMPOUND" are never explained.

**Sam (screen reader, keyboard, 200% zoom):** Pinch zoom disabled. No focus-visible styles on the tab bar or header links. ShortcutRow uses role="listbox" on a div of buttons; quick-log tabs lack role="tab" and aria-selected; range toggles lack aria-pressed; Tabs primitive has no arrow-key handling. ScoreSlider announces value 5 while displaying unset. Every alert dismiss shares the label "Dismiss". /chat has no h1 or any heading; Settings skips h1 to h3. At 200% zoom the Reintroductions header overflows at 640px effective width and the fixed 208px admin sidebar never collapses.

**Maya (foggy-day patient, 11pm, backfilling, anxious about food):** Tapping back two days then "Or pick from a list" logs to today, and she will not notice until Insights lies. Fast chevron taps can show a logged day as empty. Insights greets her with hundreds of "New pattern" cards, some pairing joint pain with good mood. Logging tomato through search puts a red "Proceed Anyway" in front of her. Reflect's Save button sits at 50% opacity with no explanation until every slider is touched.

## Minor Observations

- Body type runs 13 to 15px almost everywhere; the capture input is the only 15px text. Fraunces at text-lg on the Log date reads as a date, not a title.
- `--space-section` is defined and unused; sections use gap-2, space-y-2, mb-4, and gap-4 across tabs.
- Three teals for "brand": teal-800 wordmark, teal-700 login h1, teal-600 actives.
- Icons: Pill serves both Supplement and Medication; Activity serves yoga, stretching, sports, and other. Apple is emerald on the timeline and teal in Quick Add and admin.
- Three success phrasings for one action: "Entries saved to timeline!", "Saved 2 entries", "Logged 3 items".
- Timeline is newest-first, so a day reads backwards and the newest entry is farthest from the bar that created it.
- Chat composer clips its own two-line placeholder at 375.
- The Progress primitive maps values to width buckets to avoid inline styles while five other components use style width; the abstraction was abandoned halfway.
- Loading is always a centered spinner; the Skeleton primitive is unused. OnboardingCheck shows a full-screen "Loading..." on cold start.
- global-error.tsx is unbranded system-ui with slate hex colors.
- Admin is the most internally consistent surface. Its fixed sidebar never collapses (1085px scroll width at 375), the floating "AI Assistant" pill overlaps table rows, 505 table buttons fail contrast at 2.5:1, and deletes use native confirm().
- Favicon 404 on public pages. React hydration error #418 on nearly every authenticated load.

## Questions to Consider

- What if Insights had no "alerts" concept at all, and a pattern is either strong enough for Triggers/Helpers with a "new this week" mark, or not shown? Who is the alert stack for?
- Could the day be the unit on Log instead of the entry, one card per meal with its clarifier state, so the timeline reads like the plate Maya remembers rather than a database dump with badges?
- If Log Out, Subscription, and About moved behind the avatar, would Settings collapse into one question: which protocol, which phase, how many days?
- What would the off-protocol moment look like if it were designed as data collection ("testing tomato tells the engine something") instead of compliance? Reintroductions already frame it that way.
- What is the one-hue "Botanical Clinical" way to say high histamine (weight, density, a botanical glyph) that would make a screenshot of Pico Health identifiable with the color stripped out?
