## Priority Issues

1. **[P0] Duplicate alerts on the payoff screen.** Two identical "Brain Fog frequency" cards, same title, body, and accessible name. Root cause: the engine builds its previous-key set from the last snapshot's triggers and helpers only, so progress-milestone keys are never seen as already-known and re-fire on every run. Fix in lib/insights/engine.ts and dedupe by insightKey in the alerts route. /impeccable harden
2. **[P1] "Include 38 early signals" produces no visible change.** The rows land inside already-collapsed accordions. Auto-expand what received rows, or give early signals their own group, and announce the count. /impeccable clarify
3. **[P1] No in-app account deletion.** Settings offers export only; Terms and Privacy point at a mailbox that does not exist. Blocks App Store review under 5.1.1(v). /impeccable harden
4. **[P2] "What stands out" makes better and worse look identical.** Both arrows are teal; only the glyph direction and the word "only" differ. /impeccable colorize
5. **[P2] The one-tap path hides until the field is focused.** Shortcuts are gated behind focus, so an empty day shows 500px of blank space. /impeccable onboard
6. **[P2] Two week strips, two grammars, neither marks today.** /impeccable polish
7. **[P2] "How to read this" has no disclosure affordance.** /impeccable polish
