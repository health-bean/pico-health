## Overall Impression

The system is now coherent: one hue, one title role, one card, no traffic lights, no emoji, no hydration errors, every core route inside the phone. What remains is authorship on the payoff screen. Insights still frames observations as verdicts ("Triggers to Avoid", "Keep doing these, they're working"), lets a five-day pattern read as "100% of the time", and can show sweet potato as both a trigger and a helper on one page. The single biggest opportunity is to make Insights say three true things per week in the product's own voice.

## What's Working

- **The capture loop is designed, not assembled.** Labelled Add, rotating examples, camera that becomes send, shortcut row, streaming chips with Undo and one clarifier, a menu that flips away from the docked bar. Each piece answers a foggy-day failure.
- **The color system has a point of view and holds.** One brand hue, warm neutrals, a level ramp declared in the tokens as "not a traffic light" and honored in Badge, property cards, and Insights sections. Zero off-palette classes.
- **Copy for a frightened adult.** The privacy page's plain summary, the terms' real disclaimer, and "Logging it is the useful part. Days like this are how patterns show up." Reduced motion, branded focus rings, and pinch zoom are all in place.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Insights section framing prescribes.** "Triggers to Avoid" and "Things That Help / Keep doing these, they're working" are verdicts in the level-4 color, against the product's second principle. The percentage is larger and darker than the caveat beside it, so "100% of the time" on five days outranks "early signal". Fix in the Insights page and InsightRow: rename sections to observations ("Showed up with symptoms", "Showed up on better days"), make subtitles denominators, show "5 of 5 days" instead of a percentage for early signals, shrink the number to body size, and gate helpers on the same confidence floor as triggers so a food cannot sit in both lists. Suggested command: /impeccable clarify.

2. **[P1] Insights contradicts itself for a lapsed user.** When the window has no patterns but recent alerts exist, the cold-start card ("0 of the last 90 days logged") sits directly above "New since your last visit" with 20 items. Fix: when alerts exist but the window is empty, replace the cold-start card with "Your patterns are from before this window" and a button that switches to 180 days; and count "days tracked" on the Log strip the same way Insights counts logged days. Suggested command: /impeccable harden.

3. **[P1] Reflect sliders have no track.** The range input uses appearance-none with no runnable-track style, so each card is a circle in a blank rectangle, and an untouched slider looks identical to a 5. Fix in the score slider: draw the track, fill it in teal to the thumb, dim the thumb until a value exists, and bring the week strip and chevrons to 44px like the Log strip. Suggested command: /impeccable polish.

4. **[P1] Reintroduction verdicts are one tap and the Failed button is visually undefined.** Marking passed or failed fires immediately with no dialog or undo. The class helper is a plain join, so the Failed button gets both the primary teal fill and the danger text override. Fix: reuse the stop-trial Dialog, and give Button a real outline-danger variant instead of overriding classes. Suggested command: /impeccable harden.

5. **[P2] Food cards show taxonomy and repeat one link.** Every food card prints category and subcategory and its own "Show food properties" link; a day with fifteen entries is a uniform stack with no shape. Fix: drop the taxonomy from the card line, show the two or three non-low properties inline as level pills, keep the full list behind one expander, and group the day by meal using the mealType and time already on every entry. Suggested command: /impeccable distill.

6. **[P2] Screens have no name.** Log and Reflect use the date as the only h1, Chat has none, and the document title is "Pico Health" on every route. Fix: a small PageTitle or visually hidden h1 per tab, the date as h2, per-route metadata titles. Suggested command: /impeccable polish.

7. **[P2] Admin is unusable at phone width and uses a traffic light.** A 928px table expands the layout viewport to 1085px and stretches the bottom nav; the trigger cells use teal, warning, and danger per level instead of the ramp. Fix: collapse the fixed sidebar under the md breakpoint into the Tabs primitive, let the table scroll inside its wrapper, switch trigger cells to the level tokens. Suggested command: /impeccable adapt.

8. **[P3] System polish debt.** No favicon (404 on public pages). Dead animate-in classes in Dialog, Toast, and EntryActions with no plugin installed, so those pop without an entrance. 26 raw shadow utilities where tokens exist. Backdrops on black instead of a scrim token. Empty-state body text at 12px. Reintroductions hand-rolls its own empty state. Suggested command: /impeccable polish.

- Title Case survives in a few places ("No Reintroductions Yet", "Trigger Properties", "Daily Check-in") against sentence case elsewhere.

- Skeleton and the day view use the stock pulse animation, which the reduced-motion block does not cover.

- The "Admin" tab uses a wrench icon that reads as a second Settings.

## Questions to Consider

- If the ethic is observe, don't prescribe, why is the payoff screen's first word "Avoid"? What if Insights showed three observations for the week, chosen for what the person can act on tonight, with everything else behind "All patterns"?
- The Log's h1 is a date. Is the Log a diary (day-first, grouped by meal) or a stream (entry-first, newest on top)? The header says diary and the cards say stream.
- Does Reflect need to be a tab? Five sliders could be one end-of-day clarifier on Log, and the clarifier is already the best interaction in the app.
- Reintroductions is "a distinct workflow" hidden in Settings. Is it a tab, a banner on Log ("Day 3 of testing eggs"), or not ready to be found?
- The demo account is an admin with 366 calendar days and no recent logs, so every screenshot shows six tabs and an empty Insights window. Is the demo representing the product you sell or the one you debug?
