Strength: three ways to show it

Chosen: A · Possible triggers. Below, the same three findings from Sarah's log in each treatment. The counts are real. "Strong" and "Moderate" are the engine's own ratings, which weigh how many days there are and how big the gap is against other days. A percentage alone can't do that: 3 of 3 is also 100%.
