# Histamine — University of Barcelona group (excluding Sánchez-Pérez 2018 Foods 7:205)

## Used
1. Sánchez-Pérez S, Comas-Basté O, Veciana-Nogués MT, Latorre-Moratalla ML, Vidal-Carou MC. Low-Histamine Diets: Is the Exclusion of Foods Justified by Their Histamine Content? Nutrients. 2021;13(5):1395. DOI 10.3390/nu13051395; PMID 33919293; PMC8143338. License CC BY.
   - What it contains: a review of 10 published low-histamine diets plus **Table 1**, which gives biogenic amines in Spanish-market foods. Methods §2.2 says these come from the authors' "self-produced and updated database" (refs 11 = Sánchez-Pérez 2018 Foods; 29 = Bover-Cid et al., Encyclopedia of Food Safety 2014 chapter). It is the authors' own lab data, but not a new sampling study with its own methods section.
   - Used rows from Table 1: Sauerkraut, Eggs, Yogurt, Milk→Whole Milk, Olives, Chocolate→Dark Chocolate / Cocoa, Apricot, Grapes, Papaya, Beer, Dry-fermented sausages→Salami.
   - Skipped rows: plant foods already in the 2018 paper (eggplant, spinach, tomato, pumpkin, avocado, banana, cherry, citrus, kiwi, pineapple, plum, strawberries) to avoid duplicates; also broad categories with no single catalog match (cured/semi-cured/grated cheese, soy-fermented products, wine with type not stated, fresh white/oily fish, preserved fish, seafood, fresh meat, cured meat, nuts, red fruits, tea, soybeans).
2. Hernández-Macias S, Martín-Garcia A, Ferrer-Bustins N, et al. Inhibition of Biogenic Amines Formation in Fermented Foods by the Addition of Cava Lees. Front Microbiol. 2021;12:818565. DOI 10.3389/fmicb.2021.818565; PMID 35154043; PMC8832063. CC BY.
   - Table 2: lab-made control bread (wheat flour + sourdough + yeast), histamine nd (LOD 0.05 mg/kg) → Sourdough Bread. The experimental lab-made fermented sausages (also nd) were not used: they are not a retail product.
3. Comas-Basté O, Sánchez-Pérez S, Veciana-Nogués MT, Latorre-Moratalla M, Vidal-Carou MC. Histamine Intolerance: The Current State of the Art. Biomolecules. 2020;10(8):1181. DOI 10.3390/biom10081181; PMID 32824107; PMC7463562. CC BY.
   - Table of histamine by food category (labelled Table 1 in the text), "adapted from" the Bover-Cid 2014 encyclopedia chapter. Red wine (n=260) and white wine (n=83) are stored **excluded** (review quoting a book chapter). The chocolate row was not used: it is internally inconsistent (mean 0.58 is above the max of 0.56). The other rows are categories only.

## Looked at, not used
- PMID 40724300 — Pawul-Gruba M et al., Foods 2025;14(14):2478 (PMC12294292): ripened cheeses in **Poland**, National Veterinary Research Institute, Puławy. **Not the Barcelona group**, so out of scope for this task. It is open access and has histamine data, so another task could use it.
- PMID 41850365 — Dankar I et al., J Dairy Sci 2026 (10.3168/jds.2025-27934): Mediterranean dairy products in **Lebanon** (University of Balamand). **Not the Barcelona group**. No PMC full text; the abstract says histamine was absent in all samples, but it doesn't list the products individually.
- Toro-Funes N et al. Food Chem 2015;173:1119-24 (PMID 25466133), soybean products. Paywalled. The abstract gives only the sufu maximum (700 mg/kg) and says "not detected in non-fermented soybean products", with no per-product list.
- Latorre-Moratalla ML et al. Food Chem Toxicol 2017;99:78-85 (PMID 27856296), dry-fermented sausages risk assessment. Paywalled; the abstract gives exposure per meal, not a concentration.
- Older group papers on beers (1996), milks/cheeses (2000), anchovies (1996), and the fish/meat/wine methods papers (1989–90). All paywalled with no usable means in the abstracts.
- Front Microbiol 2012 (PMC3345612) review: the sausage table is compiled from other studies, by country, not by catalog food.
- Foods 2021 polyamines & cooking (PMC8392025), Front Nutr 2019 Polyamines in Food (PMC6637774), Nutrients 2026 polyamine intake (PMID 42197044): polyamines only, no histamine.
- Foods 2021 cava lees sausages (PMC8394411): no histamine table.

## Note for the 2018 import
Sánchez-Pérez 2018 Table 1 has **Soybean milk n=3 nd** and **Chestnuts n=2 nd**. The existing histamine-sanchez-perez-2018.json doesn't include them, even though the catalog has Soy Milk and Chestnuts. They were not added here, to keep the citations to each paper separate.
