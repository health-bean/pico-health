## Looked at, not used

- **Chai W, Liebman M. 2005, J Agric Food Chem 53:3027 (cooking methods, 10.1021/jf048128d)**: paywalled. The abstract gives only percentage losses, and ACS showed "no access" in the browser.
- **Chai W, Liebman M. 2005, J Food Compos Anal 18:723 (legumes, nuts, flours, 10.1016/j.jfca.2004.07.001)**: paywalled. The abstract gives ranges only.
- **Hönow & Hesse 2002 full text**: ScienceDirect showed a CAPTCHA, which I did not attempt. I used the values as quoted in Salgado 2023 instead.
- **Holmes RP, Kennedy M. 2000 Kidney Int (10.1046/j.1523-1755.2000.00010.x)**: paywalled. The abstract has no food values.
- **Massey LK et al. 2001 J Agric Food Chem (soy, 10.1021/jf010484y)** and **Al-Wahsh et al. 2005 (soy foods, 10.1021/jf0506378)**: abstracts give per-serving values with no serving weight, or ranges only. Horner 2005 is dry seed only. Massey 2000 "Tea oxalate" (Nutr Rev) is a commentary with no abstract.
- **McKay et al. 1995 (herbal tea)**: no abstract. Its chamomile row is already in the errata.
- **Attalla K, De S, Monga M. 2014 Urology (10.1016/j.urology.2014.03.053)**: compares websites and apps, with no lab measurement. Not usable.
- **Ohkawa H. 1985 J AOAC**: a methods paper. The abstract has no values.
- **Mahdavi R et al. 2013 Urolithiasis (tea)**: ranges only.
- **Ritter & Savage 2007 (nuts)**: reports in-vitro gastric/intestinal soluble fractions only, not total.
- **Schroder et al. 2011 (cocoa/dark chocolate)**, **Nguyễn et al. 2018 (cocoa beans)**, **Simpson 2009 (silverbeet)**, **Moreau 2009 (purslane)**, **Radek 2008**, **Ghosh Das 2012 (spices)**, **Savage 2002 (mushrooms)**, **Huang & Liebman 2015 (Chinese herbs, PMC7147225)**: dry-matter values only, with no moisture given.
- **Gélinas & Seguin 2007 (grain amaranth)**: the basis (dry or as-is) is not stated in the abstract.
- **Hönow, Gu, Hesse, Siener 2010 (green tea)** and **Siener, Seidler, Hönow 2021 (Salgado ref [63], sweet potato/okra/beans/soy/licorice)**: Siener is an author, so these are left to the other helper.
- **Salgado Table 5 rows from spectrophotometric method-development papers** (Ensafi, Jiang, Chamjangali, Xu, Safavi, Tavallali, Matsubara, Morosanova, Yan, Hassouna) and **Liu 2015 (hydroponic spinach)**: these are analytical-method papers on a few samples, mostly soluble-only. Santamaria 1999 is soluble only. Radek/Savage & Mårtensson "dried" rows have an unclear basis. Not used.
- **Salgado rows not matched to the catalog**: bilberry (not the same as cultivated blueberry), blackcurrant/redcurrant, mirabelle, muskmelon, lentil (colour not stated), soybean, amaranth leaves, Chinese kale, bitter gourd, cowpea, taro leaves, white radish (could be daikon-type), Korean green pepper, garlic chives.
- **Akhtar boiled carrot**: the abstract says 16 but Salgado quotes 26, and the paper's table did not render. Left out because of the conflict.
- **Rows already in the Norton errata** (Hönow canned peas 6.2; Savage boiled silverbeet leaves 291.1, which the errata has as 290): not re-added.
